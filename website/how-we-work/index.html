How an Xarmoured Engagement Works

Straightforward milestones, explicit permissions and deliverables your engineering team can use.

Discuss your scope ↗How we work ↗

1. Discovery call

We establish why you are testing, what matters, technical ownership and likely scope. No obligation to buy a broad security package you do not need.

2. Written scope and rules of engagement

Targets, source IPs where relevant, test accounts, allowed methods, excluded actions, reporting contacts, dates and emergency stop conditions are agreed.

3. Assessment

We map attack surfaces and evaluate high-risk controls using targeted tooling and manual validation. Material blockers and critical issues are escalated according to the agreed process.

4. Findings and walkthrough

A report explains impact and reproduction. We can discuss prioritization and what a correct fix should enforce at the trust boundary.

5. Retest

If part of the agreement, remediated findings are tested again with evidence. Any residual risk or inability to verify is recorded instead of silently marked resolved.

What determines effort?

Number of roles and tenants, sensitive workflows, API surface, build stability, test account access, integrations and operational restrictions usually matter more than the number of visible pages.
