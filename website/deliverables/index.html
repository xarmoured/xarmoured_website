What You Receive After a Penetration Test

A report should shorten the distance between discovery, engineering changes and verified resolution.

Request report details ↗How we work ↗

Executive overview

Assessment objectives, scoped assets, testing limitations, major risks and a prioritized remediation picture for decision-makers.

Technical findings

Each validated issue includes its affected component, preconditions, reproduction steps, severity rationale, technical evidence and potential impact. Where relevant, we map the finding to a CWE and technical testing standard.

Engineering-oriented fixes

We describe the control that needs to change—authorization checks, trust boundaries, data validation or infrastructure behavior—rather than simply recommending a generic patch.

Coverage and constraints

You see what was in scope, which roles or environments were exercised, what access was unavailable and where conclusions must be treated cautiously.

Retest addendum

When contracted, a follow-up document records evidence of closure, incomplete fixes and remaining weaknesses.

Note: We do not issue an invented certification or promise that any application is vulnerability-free.
