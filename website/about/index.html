What we do

Xarmoured provides scoped web application, API and Android security testing plus retesting and remediation validation. Work begins with authorization and ends with a concrete explanation of validated risks.

Why this approach

Product teams need to understand which trust boundary failed, why an attacker could exploit it and how to close it. Automated tools are useful, but they cannot replace deliberate testing of roles, tenants, workflows and business logic.

What we do not claim

Xarmoured is an early-stage practice, not a 24/7 managed security provider, compliance certification body or massive consulting agency. We do not invent client testimonials, certifications or service statistics.

Location and working model

Based in India, remote-first. Delivery logistics, commercial terms and security requirements are agreed during scoping.
