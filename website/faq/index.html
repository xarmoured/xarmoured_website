What is the difference between a vulnerability scan and a penetration test?

A scan flags potential issues using automated checks. A penetration test combines targeted automation with manual work to validate exploitability, access control and business logic in an authorized scope.

Do you need access to our source code?

Not necessarily. Most web and API engagements can be scoped as black-box or authenticated testing. Source-assisted review is a separate scope decision.

Can you test production?

Possibly, if operational risks, exclusions, test windows and emergency contacts are explicitly agreed. Representative staging is often preferable when destructive side effects are a concern.

Can you test an application with multiple organizations or tenants?

Yes, tenant separation, object ownership and role boundaries are important test areas. Provide safe test accounts in separate organizations so cross-tenant paths can be evaluated.

How long does a penetration test take?

Duration depends on attack surface, roles, complexity, access and required depth. We discuss a realistic window after reviewing your scope instead of promising a universal turnaround.

Do you provide a compliance certificate?

No. Xarmoured currently provides technical security assessments, not accreditation, legal certification or an assurance that a system has no vulnerabilities.

Will you share proof of vulnerabilities?

Validated findings include reproduction guidance and relevant evidence within confidentiality and data-minimization constraints.

Do you provide remediation guidance?

Yes. Findings explain the failed security boundary and practical remediation direction so engineers can act on the issue.

Is retesting included in every assessment?

Retesting is available, but the scope, timing and inclusion in the commercial agreement must be confirmed in the proposal.

Can we get a quote before creating test accounts?

Yes. A preliminary scope discussion does not require credentials. Test access is arranged securely only after written terms and rules of engagement.

Will you test Android and iOS?

Current mobile capability is strongest for Android applications and their backend APIs. iOS should not be assumed included without a separately confirmed scope.

Do you perform SOC, MDR or 24/7 incident response?

No. These are not part of the services advertised by Xarmoured at launch.

Do you work remotely with clients outside India?

Xarmoured is remote-first and based in India. Cross-border delivery, scheduling, contracting and any jurisdiction-specific requirements must be reviewed during scoping.

What should we send in an initial enquiry?

Share application type, environment, approximate scope, roles, target timeline and goals. Do not send credentials, sensitive data or access tokens through public forms.

Can you guarantee our application is secure after testing?

No finite assessment can guarantee the absence of vulnerabilities. It provides evidence about agreed assets, methods and conditions during the test window.
