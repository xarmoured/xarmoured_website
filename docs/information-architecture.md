# Information architecture

Primary: Services (published grouped mega-menu), Research, Approach (/methodology), Resources, Company (/about), Careers. Persistent primary conversion: Request an assessment. Utility Contact (/contact) routes to intake. Footer adds sample report, responsible disclosure, security.txt, legal pages and configured socials.

Public services are published CMS records only. Categories support Offensive Security, Security Engineering and Continuous Assurance; planned services remain draft. Empty categories are hidden. No production default service catalogue. Homepage sections are structured and switchable, with editable lifecycle lines. Research/resources use technical journal listings with search, category, severity, year and sort. Case studies/industries are optional and hidden from home until actual published records exist.

Detail pages expose only populated technical fields. Published jobs remain dynamic; closed jobs retain their detail but no application form or JobPosting markup. General applications require an explicit setting. Unknown and excess route segments return 404. Private previews are authenticated and noindex.
