API Security Testing

02 / APPROACH

How an engagement actually runs.

Five stages, in order. No stage is skipped to hit a deadline.

01

Scope & threat model

We define what's actually in play and what an attacker would realistically go after — not a generic checklist copied across clients.

02

Active testing

Manual exploitation first, tooling second. We chain low-severity issues together the way a real attacker would, instead of reporting them in isolation.

03

Validation

Every finding is proven exploitable before it goes into the report. Nothing is raw scanner output passed off as a confirmed vulnerability.

04

Reporting & debrief

A walkthrough with your engineering team, not just a PDF — with clear severity ratings and reproduction steps they can act on immediately.

05

Retest

We verify your fixes within 30 days of the final report, at no extra cost, and confirm closure in writing for your audit trail.
